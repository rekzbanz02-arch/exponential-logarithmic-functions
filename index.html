4. Logarithmic functions - PDF pages 26-34
Meaning of a Logarithm
log_b(x)=y
It asks: to what exponent must b be raised to obtain x?
Equivalent Exponential Form
log_b(x)=y ⇔ bʸ=x
The base remains the same.
Parts
b=base, x=argument, y=exponent/logarithm
Restrictions: b>0, b≠1, x>0.
Common Logarithm
log₁₀(x)=log(x)
Use the calculator's LOG key.
Natural Logarithm
logₑ(x)=ln(x)
Use the calculator's LN key.
Graph Relationship
Exponential and logarithmic functions are inverses.
Page 34 displays their reflection across y=x.
Conversion trick: in log_b(c)=a, keep b as the base and rewrite it as bᵃ=c.
